project kotiloplay

cozy music-companion concept

KotiloPlay is a cozy music-companion concept built around an illustrated house and garden. Visitors can explore clickable rooms, enjoy animated scenery, and play music through YouTube.

Features

  • Illustrated house and garden
    • A welcoming visual world created with CSS and SVG.
    • Illustrated rooms and outdoor spaces that give the app its own personality.
  • Clickable room links
    • Explore different areas of the house and garden through interactive links.
    • Each room creates a clear, playful way to move through the experience.
  • Animated scenery
    • Subtle animations make the illustrated environment feel alive.
    • Motion is used to support the cozy mood without overwhelming the interface.
  • YouTube music player
    • Play music directly through YouTube integration.
    • Connects the visual experience with a simple way to listen to music.

Tech Stack

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Vite
  • CSS
  • SVG
  • YouTube integration
