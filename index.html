A little about me

Careful thinking.
A curious mind.

Before software development, I spent over a decade working in Finnish pharmacies and online retail. That experience taught me to work accurately, understand people’s needs, and pay attention to the details.

Now I bring that same care to web and mobile development. I enjoy turning ideas into clear interfaces, working through the logic behind them, and testing what I build on real devices.

I’m still learning, building, and finding better ways to make things work.

I like building things that feel considered—small details, clear interactions, and interfaces that make people feel welcome.
